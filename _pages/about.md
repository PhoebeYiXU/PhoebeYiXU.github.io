I am an Associate Professor at the [USC-SJTU Institute of Cultural and Creative Industry](https://icci.sjtu.edu.cn/en/) (ICCI), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I direct research at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **Cognitive Psychology**.

My work examines how AI agents and algorithmic platforms reshape human cognition, meta-cognition, and social behavior in high-stakes environments. Current focus areas include *Sense of Agency in human-AI collaboration*, *Algorithmic Fairness and Trust*, and *agent-augmented education* for cognitive growth and digital well-being (also labelled "agentic education" in industry discourse).

I received my Ph.D. in Social Psychology from [the Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), M.A. in Developmental Psychology from [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/), and B.S. in Electronics Engineering from [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/). Before academia, I worked as a Procurement Manager at General Electric (GE) China, managing complex industrial supply chains for Wind Energy (2003–2006).

# 📚 Teaching & Curriculum Design

I advocate for an *Agentic Education* paradigm, viewing the classroom as a real-world testbed for studying and advancing human-AI symbiosis. My teaching practice bridges technical AI capabilities with humanistic insight, preparing students to become active creators in the AI era rather than passive users.

- **AI: Its Nature and Future** — An interdisciplinary core course exploring the fundamentals of both Artificial and Natural Intelligence. It integrates computer science and psychology to examine the evolution of machine intelligence, cognitive foundations, multimodal systems, large language models, and the ethical, social, and philosophical boundaries of AI-driven technologies. The course balances technical depth with critical humanistic reflection, helping students develop well-rounded AI literacy.

- **Digital Cultural and Creative Industries Research II** — A project-based learning (PBL) course focused on industry-oriented research in digital creative sectors, including AIGC (AI-Generated Content), virtual humans, immersive media, and digital entertainment. The curriculum emphasizes business model innovation, frontier technology application, and leadership skills required in AI-integrated creative industries. **Course outputs are publicly recapped on the [ICCI WeChat 公众号](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/) (e.g., industry field-visit reports with China Literature, Shanghai Jiushi Performance, and Double Happiness), reaching a broad professional audience and serving as a public-facing portfolio of the course.**
